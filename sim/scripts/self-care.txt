# Self-care while charging (#24): on battery nothing tops the needs up; on USB, once
# the pet has been untouched for 10 minutes, the first need under 40 goes back to 50
# once a minute. The "self-care:" lines and the STATS replies are in serial.log.
#   tools/sim.sh --script self-care --out shots/self-care
# The same with an AXP2101 that never answers (USB reads as present): self-care-no-pmu.

battery 60

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s

# on battery, untouched for 22 minutes: food falls under 40 and stays there
wait 22m
serial STATS

# plug in and wake the screen: the touch starts the 10 minutes
usb on
press pwr
wait 1s
shot plugged-in
wait 9m
serial STATS

# the tenth untouched minute: food back to 50, then the next need each minute
wait 2m
serial STATS
wait 10m
serial STATS

# wake the screen again to see the pet and its bars
press pwr
wait 1s
shot after-self-care
