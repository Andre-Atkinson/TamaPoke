# Touch works while an attention call lights the screen. The bug: the pet called (a need under
# 25: "Needs a bath!", the "!" bubble, the poops), the timed-out screen lit for 15 s, and every
# tap was ignored until PWR, because the auto touch lock read only screenOff, which a call's
# "peek" leaves true (attention.h). Now the lock lets go while the call lights the screen
# (touchScreenDark, touchlock.h), and the first tap does what it taps (touchOnlyWakes), so one
# tap on the bath answers a bath call. Left untouched, the screen goes dark again and the lock
# comes back after its usual 10 s. A screen turned off with PWR gets no lit window: its call
# chirps in the dark and the touch stays locked.
#   tools/sim.sh --script touch-during-call --serial-log calls.log --out shots/
# The 1.75C only: the original 1.75's screen never turns off by default and it has no touch
# lock, so its calls brighten a lit screen instead, and on a lit screen the pet's idle life
# brings the call earlier; tools/gate.sh runs this on both boards, and the 1.75 skips it.
battery 80
board 175c

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg (the clock is 2026-11-13 10:00)
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s
# the screen times out at 60 s untouched, and the touch locks 10 s later
wait 80s
expect screen off: idle 60000
expect touch lock

# A. hygiene's call (~840 s) lights the timed-out screen for 15 s, and the lock lets go
wait 758s
expect call hygiene: peek chirp
expect touch unlock
shot 1-hygiene-call
# one tap on the bath button during the call bathes the pet (it used to do nothing)
tap 326 390
wait 6s
serial STATS
expect lim=100
shot 2-bathed-from-the-call

# B. the bath's tap turned the screen on for real; it times out again (~910 s) and locks. Food's
# call (~28 min) is left alone: the lock lets go for its window, then the screen is dark again
# and the lock comes back 10 s after the window ends
wait 75s
expect touch lock
wait 763s
expect call food: peek chirp
expect touch unlock
expect-not touch lock
wait 30s
expect touch lock

# C. PWR on, then off: a screen turned off with PWR gets no lit window. Joy's call (~35 min)
# chirps in the dark and the touch stays locked
press pwr
wait 2s
press pwr
wait 15s
expect touch unlock
expect touch lock
wait 420s
expect call joy: dark chirp
expect-not touch unlock
