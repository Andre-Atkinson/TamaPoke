# The berry garden (#115, garden.h): the pot at the right-hand end of the ground (tap box
# x 360-458, y 237-310; the pot itself at x 399-448, y 242-308).
#   A. on the egg screen the pot takes its own taps: plant, water; a ripe plant waits
#   B. hatched: a pick, the berry's hop and the heart; a second tap while it eats waits;
#      three berries and the pot is empty
#   C. a wilted stalk clears and the pot plants again
#   D. rain on screen waters it; the thirsty and ripe lines (shots only: the say step logs
#      nothing)
#   E. it stays through an ending (BYE): the new egg finds the plant as it was
#   F. watered time grows it (4 h: a sprout), with the screen dark
#   tools/sim.sh --script garden --out shots/        (and --lang pt for the speech lines)
clock 2026-11-13 10:00
battery 80

# A. new game: pick Charmander; the egg is on screen
wait 1s
tap 233 220
wait 500ms
tap 424 290
expect garden: planted *
shot 1-egg-planting
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: watered
serial GARDEN ripe wet blue
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: ripe, the pet can't eat now
expect-not garden: picked
serial GARDEN?
expect garden stage=ripe berry=blue left=3 wet=1 *

# B. hatch it: a berry for the pet, with its hop to the pet and the heart
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: picked blue, 2 left
shot 2-pick-hop
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: ripe, the pet can't eat now
wait 3s
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: picked blue, 1 left
wait 3s
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: picked blue, 0 left
serial GARDEN?
expect garden stage=empty *

# C. a wilted stalk clears with a tap, and the next tap plants
wait 3s
serial GARDEN withered
shot 3-withered
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: cleared
serial GARDEN?
expect garden stage=empty *
tap 424 290
expect-once garden: planted *

# D. rain on screen waters a dry plant at once; the lines
serial GARDEN leaves dry green
serial GARDEN?
expect garden stage=leaves berry=green left=0 wet=0 *
serial SCENE meadow rain
wait 2s
serial GARDEN?
expect garden stage=leaves berry=green left=0 wet=1 *
shot 4-rain
serial SCENE auto
serial GARDEN flower wilting
say thirsty
shot 5-thirsty
serial GARDEN ripe
say ripe
shot 6-ripe

# E. an ending keeps the garden
serial GARDEN flower wet red
serial BYE
wait 12s
serial GARDEN?
expect garden stage=flower berry=red *
shot 7-after-ending

# F. watered time grows it, with the screen dark too (serial only from here: the screen goes
# dark and the 1.75C locks its touch): a seed with its 12 h of water is a sprout after 4 h
# (rain can only add water)
serial GARDEN seed wet
wait 4h10m
serial GARDEN?
expect garden stage=sprout *
