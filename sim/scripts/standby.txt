# The standby clock (#60, standby.h): plugged in and left alone, a dim clock with the date
# and the weather takes the place of screen-off (the 1.75C turns its screen off at 60 s;
# the original 1.75's default is Never, so its clock comes at 90 s). A touch brings the pet
# back; unplugged or asleep, the screen's usual rules apply. Its "standby clock" lines are
# in serial.log, and SCENE? ends with standby=<0|1>.
#
# After the clock itself: a gallery left open when the clock came up (the touch shows the
# pet, not a frozen clock), PWR and the pocket lock (1.75C) while the clock shows, the
# season's falling petals only at the full frame rate, and settings left open across a
# bedtime (the views are put away when the clock comes up, so the pet goes to bed; the
# clock does not stay lit all night). Where the two boards differ it is said in the step.

usb on
charging on
battery 60
clock 2026-11-13 10:00
wait 1s
tap 233 220
wait 500ms
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s
shot standby-0-pet

# left alone: the clock (by 100 s on both boards)
wait 100s
shot standby-1-clock
wait 3m
shot standby-2-clock-moved

# a touch (the first only wakes): the pet again
tap 233 233
wait 1s
shot standby-3-pet-again

# the gallery left open: the clock comes up over it and puts it away, so a touch shows the
# pet, not a frozen frame of the clock (the grid draws only when it changes)
swipe right
wait 1s
shot standby-3a-gallery
wait 100s
shot standby-3b-gallery-clock
tap 233 300
wait 2s
shot standby-3c-gallery-touch-pet
wait 5s
shot standby-3d-gallery-later

# PWR while the clock shows turns the screen fully off ("standby clock: off"), and again on
wait 100s
press pwr
wait 2s
shot standby-3e-pwr-off
press pwr
wait 1s
shot standby-3f-pwr-on

# the pocket lock (1.75C: three PWR presses; the original 1.75 has none, so there the three
# presses only turn its screen off, on, off, and the second three on, off, on): the clock
# gives way to the lock's dark screen, and unlocking shows the pet
wait 100s
press pwr 3
wait 300ms
shot standby-3g-lock-hint
wait 3s
press pwr 3
wait 1500ms
shot standby-3h-unlocked

# the season's petals fall only at the full frame rate: unplugged and untouched the screen
# dims (the 1.75C at 30 s, the original 1.75 at 90 s), and a dimmed frame has the sky but
# no petals. The shot at 40 s is dimmed on the 1.75C and lit on the 1.75 (petals); the one at
# 100 s is dark on the 1.75C and dimmed on the 1.75 (none)
serial SCENE meadow clear spring
tap 233 233
wait 1s
shot standby-3i-petals-lit
usb off
charging off
wait 40s
shot standby-3j-dimmed-40s
wait 60s
shot standby-3k-dimmed-100s
tap 233 233
wait 1s
usb on
charging on
serial SCENE auto

# unplugged: no clock. The 1.75C's screen goes off at 60 s (its touch lock follows 10 s
# later, so a touch at 62 s still wakes it); the 1.75 (Never) stays lit
usb off
charging off
wait 62s
shot standby-4-on-battery
tap 233 233
wait 1s

# settings left open across a bedtime. The care page's bedtime is set (the first preset,
# 21:00), the settings opened and left alone at 20:5x. When the clock comes up it puts the
# open views away, so bedtime is not held and the pet goes to bed on USB, the clock gone
# ("standby clock: off"); with the views left open the pet would stay up and the clock
# would stay lit all night. Plugged in and asleep there is no clock either (the 1.75C's
# screen goes dark at 60 s, the original 1.75 dims): CARE? says inbed=1, and BOOT lights the
# screen to show the pet asleep.
usb on
charging on
press boot
wait 10h35m
tap 233 233
wait 1s
swipe up
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
tap 233 192
wait 1s
shot standby-5a-care-bedtime
swipe up
wait 1s
swipe down
wait 1s
shot standby-5b-settings
serial TIME
wait 100s
shot standby-5c-clock-over-settings
wait 15m
serial CARE?
serial TIME
shot standby-5d-asleep
press boot
wait 1s
