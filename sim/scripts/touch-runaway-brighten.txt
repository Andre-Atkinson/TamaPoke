# touch-runaway-peek.txt's case on a screen that is still lit: while the run-away button is up,
# the first tap on any call's window only wakes, also when the call brightened a dimmed screen
# rather than lighting a timed-out one. The button is one tap with no dialog and lies across the
# pet, so a tap meant for the pet must not end it. So: sickness on, the pet neglected until the
# button shows, the screen left to dim but not time out, and SICK ON makes it call (a brighten).
# The first tap only wakes the screen; the next one is the player's.
#   tools/sim.sh --script touch-runaway-brighten --serial-log calls.log --out shots/
# The 1.75C only (its screen dims at 30 s and times out at 60 s; the 1.75's never times out, so
# the same timings would not line up); tools/gate.sh runs it on both boards and the 1.75 skips it.
battery 80
board 175c

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg (the clock is 2026-11-13 10:00)
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s

# the Care page's Sickness setting on (the stat card, five pages along), then the card closed
swipe up
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
tap 233 304
expect sickness on
swipe up
wait 1s

# neglect it until the run-away button shows, and let the screen dim but not time out
serial ABANDON
wait 40s
expect-not screen off

# sickness calls (a brighten: the screen is still lit, so no peek)
serial SICK ON
wait 2s
expect call sick: brighten chirp
expect-not call sick: peek
shot 1-sick-brighten

# the first tap lands on the run-away button but only wakes the screen
tap 233 205
wait 2s
expect-not run-away: tapped
shot 2-first-tap-only-wakes

# the next tap is the player's: the run-away button works
tap 233 205
wait 1s
expect run-away: tapped
