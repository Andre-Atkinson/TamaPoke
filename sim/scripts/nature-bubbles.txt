# Natures in play (#86): the line a pet says by itself right after it hatches (it names its
# nature), every nature's three lines by day, two by night, the longest beside the clock
# badge, and a tidy pet strolling clear of the poops.
# The steps mean the same on both boards: no step waits long enough for the screen to dim.
# A manual check: run it in each language on both boards and look at the shots and the
# "say ..." lines in the serial log; nothing compares them automatically.
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --lang pt --script nature-bubbles --out shots/pt/
# "say LINE" shows a line now (simulator only, not logged); "nature NAME" sets the nature
# (simulator only). The nature is rolled at the hatch, so the first shot sets it straight
# after, before the first bubble comes (3 s after the pet screen is free).
clock none
battery 80

# a new game with no time: pick Charmander, skip the clock screen (the badge stays), hatch.
# The first bubble comes by itself and names the nature: "say playful-1" in serial.log.
wait 1s
tap 233 220
wait 1s
swipe up
serial HATCH
nature playful
wait 5s
shot 01-hatch-line-by-itself-badge

# beside the clock badge, the longest line in each language (tidy-3)
say tidy-3
wait 300ms
shot 02-badge-longest-line

# back to 10:00. A tidy pet with poops on the ground: its strolls keep to the right part of its
# range (x 230 and on), clear of them ("poops N", simulator only). A tap on empty sky (x 400)
# keeps the screen lit; "idle walk" starts a stroll, and the shot catches it on its way or
