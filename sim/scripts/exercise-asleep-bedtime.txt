# The pet falls asleep at bedtime while the stat card's exercise choice is up (#89): choosing
# an exercise then keeps the card open on its Fitness page with the "it's sleeping" note,
# instead of closing it and doing nothing. The serial log shows "TRAIN: asleep". For both
# boards and both languages; leave out --clock, which would replace the clock below:
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --lang en --script exercise-asleep-bedtime --out shots/
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175 --lang pt --script exercise-asleep-bedtime --out shots/
# Timing: the loop reads the clock about every 30 s from boot (bedtime acts on those reads),
# so with the clock at 20:59:15 the read at ~30 s sees 20:59:45 and the one at ~60 s sees
# 21:00:15, which puts the pet to sleep. The choice is open from ~16 s; PWR off and on at
# ~50 s keeps the screen lit (the 1.75C turns it off after 60 s untouched and dims it at
# 30 s, and a tap on a dimmed screen only wakes it).
clock 2026-11-16 20:59:15

tap 233 220

tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s

swipe left
tap 233 172
shot 60-bedtime-21

# back to Fitness, TRAIN: the choice
swipe right
