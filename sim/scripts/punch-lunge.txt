# The rhythm punch's lunge (#90, punchlunge.h): a landed punch plays the pet's own ATTACK
# from its strike, standing on its feet at IDLE's size, and the pet is back on IDLE by
# 0.7 s; a tap that doesn't land shows nothing. Charmeleon (#5), whose ATTACK the renderer
# already draws at IDLE's scale, then Charmander (#4), whose ATTACK it would draw a size
# smaller (drawPmdActM). For both boards, by day and by night:
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --lang en --script punch-lunge --out shots/
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175 --lang en --clock "2026-11-16 21:00" --script punch-lunge --out shots/
# Timing: a tap is 80 ms down then 400 ms settling, and a hold's shot is taken at its end
# with the finger still down. A training frame is 85 ms, and a tap is drawn from the frame
# after the one it was read in, so a strike's shot is 170 ms on. A punch lands when the
# finger goes down; the session starts when the tap that chose it lifts.
# Since #126 a session runs until its miss: until the first
# punch it is a warm-up, where the swings repeat at 1.5 s (windows 400-1100 ms into each)
# and an off-beat tap does nothing, so the first swing is let pass and the second one's tap
# comes early. From the first punch, at the second swing (window 1900-2600 ms from the
# start), each swing is 35 ms shorter: the third from 3000 ms (window 3394-4070), the fourth
# from 4465 (4854-5506), the fifth from 5895 (6278-6906). Letting the fifth pass is the miss.

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg, then Charmeleon
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s
serial SPEC 5
wait 2s

# the card's second page, TRAIN, the punch (about 400 ms in when the tap settles)
swipe up
swipe left
tap 233 320
tap 233 140
# ~1.5 s: the second swing, before its window (1.9 s), in the warm-up: nothing lands, the pet
# stays on IDLE
wait 1100ms
hold 233 240 60ms 01-c5-early
# ~1.96 s: the first punch lands, and 170 ms on the pet strikes
hold 233 240 170ms 02-c5-strike
# ~3.53 s: the second punch; 240 ms on, the strike runs on
wait 1s
hold 233 240 240ms 03-c5-mid
# ~5.07 s: the third punch; 700 ms on, the pet is back on IDLE
wait 900ms
hold 233 240 700ms 04-c5-idle-again
# the fifth swing passes with no punch: the miss (~6.97 s), its result, then back to the pet
wait 6s

# Charmander: the same punches
serial SPEC 4
wait 2s
swipe up
swipe left
tap 233 320
tap 233 140
wait 1100ms
hold 233 240 60ms 11-c4-early
hold 233 240 170ms 12-c4-strike
wait 1s
hold 233 240 240ms 13-c4-mid
wait 900ms
hold 233 240 700ms 14-c4-idle-again
shot 15-c4-count

# Mid-session the sprite changes (SPEC 25, Pikachu): ensureMon() reloads it and the punch
# plans its lunge again from the new one (exerciseSpriteChanged, drawPunch). ~6.42 s: the
# fourth punch lands, in the fifth swing, and Pikachu strikes on its feet at its own size.
serial SPEC 25
wait 200ms
hold 233 240 170ms 16-c25-strike
