# The first tap on a call's lit screen acts (touch-during-call.txt), except while the run-away
# button is up: that button is one tap with no dialog and lies across the pet, so a tap meant for
# the pet must not end it. A sickness call can peek then (a need's call can't: it called long
# before), so: sickness on, the pet neglected until the button shows, the screen timed out, and
# SICK ON makes it call. The first tap only wakes the screen; the next one is the player's.
# touch-runaway-brighten.txt is the same on a dimmed screen the call brightens.
#   tools/sim.sh --script touch-runaway-peek --serial-log calls.log --out shots/
# The 1.75C only (the touch lock and the timed-out screen); tools/gate.sh runs it on both boards
# and the 1.75 skips it.
battery 80
board 175c

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg (the clock is 2026-11-13 10:00)
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s

# the Care page's Sickness setting on (the stat card, five pages along), then the card closed
swipe up
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
tap 233 304
expect sickness on
swipe up
wait 1s

# neglect it until the run-away button shows, then let the screen time out and the touch lock
serial ABANDON
wait 120s
expect screen off
expect touch lock

# sickness calls (a peek: the screen lights, the touch lock lets go)
serial SICK ON
wait 2s
expect call sick: peek chirp
expect touch unlock
shot 1-sick-peek

# the first tap lands on the run-away button but only wakes the screen
tap 233 205
wait 2s
expect-not run-away: tapped
shot 2-first-tap-only-wakes

# the next tap is the player's: the run-away button works
tap 233 205
wait 1s
expect run-away: tapped
