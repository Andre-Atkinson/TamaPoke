# Quiet hours for the creature's own sounds (#44): the evolve chime, the run-away warning,
# the hourly level-up and a medal that time earned are spent silently in quiet hours (a
# stale clock, or 22:00-07:00 with bedtime off) and do not sound later; in the daytime they
# sound as before.
# A manual check: run it on both boards and read the "alert ..." and "call ..." lines in
# the serial log; nothing compares them automatically. The simulator plays no audio.
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --script quiet-alerts --serial-log alerts.log --out shots/
# Expected, in order:
#   A. stale clock at noon:   alert evolve: silent (quiet)
#   B. 23:00, bedtime off:     alert medal: silent (quiet), call ...: silent (quiet),
#                              alert level 11: silent (quiet), alert run-away: silent (quiet)
#   C. next day, 10:00:        no run-away line while its button stays (spent at night), then
#                              alert evolve: sound, and after a new neglect alert run-away: sound
# The steps mean the same on both boards; the shots only show the buttons (the 1.75C turns
# the screen off after 60 s, so PWR is pressed only where its state is known on the 1.75C).
