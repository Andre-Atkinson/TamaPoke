# The Play minigames are endless (#127): each game is played to a few points, then missed, and
# the miss ends it at once and pays what was done. For both boards, both languages, by day and
# by night:
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --lang en --script endless-games --out shots/
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175 --lang pt --clock "2026-11-16 21:00" --script endless-games --out shots/
# The games' dice decide where the ball, the pet, the berry or the next note is, so the
# `gameplay point` and `gameplay miss` steps ask the game where to touch (sim/script.h). Each
# section plays one game, looks at its screen with some points (the score, the record and the
# pay step "x2" or "x3" under it), and misses: a dropped ball, a good berry that reaches the
# ground, a wrong bush, a wrong note. The miss ends the game that frame: its result screen
# comes at once, with the line "Play: GAME score=N speed=+M" paid once (the pay is
# gameSpeedPay: keepy-uppy 8 hits pay 1, Berry Catch 12 points 1, Peekaboo 14 hits 3, Berry
# Song a 6-note tune 3). Before the miss nothing is paid ("expect-not Play:"). The Play button
# is at (202, 404) and its strip's Poke Ball at (169, 320) (#13); a game shows its title card
# for 1.2 s, then plays, and its result stays up for 4 s.

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s
serial SCENE auto clear auto

# 1. Keepy-uppy: 8 hits, then the ball is left to drop
game keepy
tap 202 404
tap 169 320
gameplay point 8
shot 01-keepy-play
expect-not Play:
gameplay miss
shot 02-keepy-result
expect-once Play:
expect Play: keepy score=8 speed=+1
wait 5s
