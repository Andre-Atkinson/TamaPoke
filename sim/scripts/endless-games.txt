# The Play minigames are endless (#127): each game is played to a few points, then missed, and
# the miss ends it at once and pays what was done. For both boards, both languages, by day and
# by night:
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --lang en --script endless-games --out shots/
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175 --lang pt --clock "2026-11-16 21:00" --script endless-games --out shots/
# The games' dice decide where the ball, the pet, the berry or the next note is, so the
# `gameplay point` and `gameplay miss` steps ask the game where to touch (sim/script.h). Each
# section plays one game, looks at its screen with some points (the score, the record and the
# pay step "x2" or "x3" under it), and misses: a dropped ball, a good berry that reaches the
# ground, a wrong bush, a wrong note. The miss ends the game that frame: its result screen
# comes at once, with the line "Play: GAME score=N speed=+M" paid once (the pay is
# gameSpeedPay: keepy-uppy 20 hits pay 5, Berry Catch 30 points 6, Peekaboo 21 hits 6, Berry
# Song a 9-note tune 6; the old pay, a fifth of the normalised score, would give 4, 4, 4 and 4). Before the miss nothing is paid ("expect-not Play:"). The Play button
# is at (202, 404) and its strip's Poke Ball at (169, 320) (#13); a game shows its title card
# for 1.2 s, then plays, and its result stays up for 4 s. Sections 5 and 6 leave by the
# exit chip (#129).

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s
serial SCENE auto clear auto

# 1. Keepy-uppy: 20 hits, then the ball is left to drop
game keepy
tap 202 404
tap 169 320
gameplay point 20
shot 01-keepy-play
expect-not Play:
gameplay miss
shot 02-keepy-result
expect-once Play:
expect Play: keepy score=20 speed=+5
wait 5s

# 2. Berry Catch: 30 berries caught (the rotten ones come after the fifth), then a good berry
# the pet isn't at
game catch
tap 202 404
tap 169 320
gameplay point 30
shot 03-catch-play
expect-not Play:
gameplay miss
shot 04-catch-result
expect-once Play:
expect Play: catch score=30 speed=+6
wait 5s

# 3. Peekaboo: 21 hits, then a tap on the wrong bush
game peek
tap 202 404
tap 169 320
gameplay point 21
shot 05-peek-play
expect-not Play:
gameplay miss
shot 06-peek-result
expect-once Play:
expect Play: peek score=21 speed=+6
wait 5s

# 4. Berry Song: the first 2-note tune, then each one a note longer up to 9 notes, then a wrong note
game song
tap 202 404
tap 169 320
gameplay point 8
shot 07-song-play
expect-not Play:
gameplay miss
shot 08-song-result
expect-once Play:
expect Play: song score=9 speed=+6
wait 5s

# 5. Leaving a played game by the exit chip (hold it for 1 s) pays what was done, as a miss
# does (#129, R1-2): Berry Song, 8 notes' worth of points, the tune playing, then the exit.
# The same line and the same pay as section 4, and the result screen.
game song
tap 202 404
tap 169 320
gameplay point 8
expect-not Play:
hold 233 40 1100ms
shot 09-song-exit-result
expect-once Play:
expect Play: song score=9 speed=+6
wait 5s
