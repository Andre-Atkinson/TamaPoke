# Holidays and the birthday (#26, holiday.h): every special day's decorations by day and by
# night, with the greeting coming by itself (the player's name, and no name); the birthday
# (alone, beside the Christmas tree, and 29 Feb on 28 Feb in a year without one); Christmas
# in the rain and at the beach; and the cases with no decorations or no greeting: no trusted
# clock, quiet hours, an ordinary day, a farewell.
# A manual check: run it in each language on both boards and look at the shots and the
# "say ..." lines in the serial log; nothing compares them automatically.
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --lang pt --script holidays --out shots/pt/
# TIME moves the clock to each day (the decorations follow the clock at once). SCENE keeps
# the pet in the meadow in clear weather, so the shots compare. The greeting takes the place
# of the next bubble that comes: PWR turns the screen off (it is on: just touched), two
# minutes pass so a bubble is due, PWR turns it on again, and 3 s later the bubble comes.
# The steps mean the same on both boards (PWR is only pressed with the screen just touched).
# The name and the birthdays are placeholders.
clock none
battery 80

# new game with no time: pick Charmander, skip the clock screen (the badge stays), hatch.
# RTCSET puts the clock on Christmas morning without setting it: still asking, so no tree
# and no greeting (the first bubble comes by itself, an ordinary line)
wait 1s
tap 233 220
wait 1s
swipe up
serial HATCH
serial OWNER Sam
serial RTCSET 1798192800
serial SCENE meadow clear auto
wait 5s
shot 01-no-clock-no-decorations
serial BDAY?

# Christmas Day, the clock set: the tree, its lights and a little snow, and the greeting
serial TIME 1798192800

press pwr

serial BDAY?
press pwr
serial TIME 1798232400

press pwr
wait 3500ms
shot 03-christmas-night-greeting
# 23:00 is quiet hours (bedtime off: 22:00-07:00): the tree stays up, the bubble is an
# ordinary line
press pwr
serial TIME 1798239600
wait 2m

wait 3500ms
shot 04-christmas-quiet-hours-no-greeting
# in the rain the weather's own drops fall, not the festive snow; and the tree at the beach
serial TIME 1798192800
serial SCENE meadow rain auto
wait 1s
shot 05-christmas-rain
serial SCENE beach clear auto
wait 1s
shot 06-christmas-beach
serial SCENE meadow clear auto
# no name: the greeting says only the day
serial OWNER
say christmas
wait 300ms
shot 07-christmas-no-name
