# Walks (#117): the 1.75C's step counter (the QMI8658's step engine), what it makes of today's
# steps, the walk treats, the walking ribbon and the speech bubble after a walk, and the pages that
# show them. The simulator's chip counts only when the script says so (`steps N`, sim/script.h);
# the original 1.75 has no step counter, so what the 1.75C counts is under `board 175c` and what
# the 1.75 does (nothing) under `board 175`: tools/gate.sh runs every script with a check on both
# boards. The shots are the Fitness and medals pages, as the board has them.
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175c --lang pt --script walks --out shots/
#   tools/sim.sh --board 175 --lang en --script walks --out shots/
# The pet reads the chip once a minute (Pet::walkMinute), so each `steps` is followed by a wait
# of a little over a minute. `serial STEPS?` prints engine on, the chip's count, today's steps, the
# day, the treats banked, the treats earned today and whether counting is open now.
