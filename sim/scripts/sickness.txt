# Sickness and medicine (#15). The serial SICK hook stands in for the 6 hours in the red that
# make the pet sick, and its minutes for a day left sick:
#   A. the Care page's fourth row switches sickness on
#   B. sick: one call, the HURT pose, the status line, the thermometer bubble, the medicine
#      button (a long press on it is not one on the pet), the sick line said by itself, and
#      the Progress page's "Get well to evolve"
#   C. a banner holds the buttons over the pet back; then the medicine: the pill, "Feeling
#      better!", and the evolve button it was holding back, which a banner holds back too
#   D. it says so in a speech bubble; the warning call, halfway to running away; asleep it
#      calls no one, not even once it wakes
#   E. left sick a day it runs away, but only once someone looks at the screen
#   F. a call's light alone is not someone looking: it stays until a touch
#   tools/sim.sh --script sickness --out shots/          (and --lang pt for the texts)
battery 80

# new game: pick Charmander, hatch the egg (the clock is 2026-11-13 10:00, outside quiet hours)
wait 1s
tap 233 220
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s

# A. off until the Care page turns it on: SICK says so
serial SICK ON
expect ERR sickness is off*
swipe up
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
tap 233 304
expect sickness on
shot 1-care-sickness-on
swipe up

# B. sick, at level 20 (ready to evolve but for the sickness): the call goes out once
serial LVL 20
serial SICK ON
wait 2s
expect-once call sick: brighten chirp
shot 2-sick
# 3 s on the medicine button is not a long press on the pet: no "release?" (nor a pill). On
# the pet's body, below the buttons' place, it is; the dialog closes by itself after 10 s.
hold 233 205 3500ms
expect-not release: asked
expect-not medicine: taken
hold 233 285 3500ms
expect-once release: asked
wait 11s
# The bubble loop says the sick line by itself (no "say" step): bubbles come 1 to 2 minutes
# apart, so taps off the pet and the buttons (x 400, y 140) keep the screen lit and undimmed
# until one does.
tap 400 140
wait 25s
tap 400 140
wait 25s
tap 400 140
wait 25s
tap 400 140
wait 25s
tap 400 140
wait 25s
expect say sick
swipe up
swipe left
swipe left
swipe left
shot 3-progress-get-well
swipe up

# C. a banner first: three days' care in a row (CAREDAY moves the clock a day and pats) brings
# the "3 DAY STREAK!" banner, then any medal that care earned. While a banner shows, the
# buttons over the pet wait, drawn and tapped alike: a tap where the medicine button goes is a
# pat. Once the banners are over, the same tap gives the medicine (the button is at y 176-234):
# the pill, then the evolve button it was holding back.
serial CAREDAY
serial CAREDAY
serial CAREDAY
wait 300ms
shot 4-banner-holds-the-button
# the sim's swipe is a straight line, so it cannot make a stroke (two turns); a rest of 300 ms,
# then a slide of 80 px over 2.4 s, is the hold-then-drag, the other gesture only the pet
# gets. Over the button's place, with the banner up, the pet is all there is: it drags.
swipe 150 205 230 205 2400ms
expect PET drag
tap 233 205
expect-not medicine: taken
wait 15s
tap 233 205
expect medicine: taken
wait 500ms
shot 5-taking-medicine
wait 3s
serial CARE?
expect * sickness=1 sick=0 germ=0 heal=0 sickmin=0
shot 6-better-evolve-button
# The evolve button waits under a banner too: four more days' care in a row (CAREDAY) make
# the 7-day streak, its banner and its medal's. A tap on the evolve button's place is a pat
# then; after the banners, the same tap opens the evolve/keep dialog, which closes by itself.
serial CAREDAY
serial CAREDAY
serial CAREDAY
serial CAREDAY
wait 300ms
tap 233 204
expect-not evolve: asked
wait 10s
tap 233 204
expect-once evolve: asked
wait 13s

# D. sick again, a minute short of the warning: the "sick" call, then the warning. A tap off
# the pet and the buttons (x 400, y 140) keeps the screen lit: the 1.75C turns it off at 60 s.
serial SICK ON 719
wait 2s
expect-once call sick: *
# the same slide, now over the medicine button (no banner): the button is not the pet, so no
# drag, and a drag that ended on it is no tap either (no pill)
swipe 150 205 230 205 2400ms
expect-not PET drag
expect-not medicine: taken
tap 400 140
wait 40s
tap 400 140
wait 1s
say sick
wait 300ms
shot 7-says-so
wait 24s
expect-once call sick-warning: *
serial CARE?
expect * sickness=1 sick=1 * sickmin=72*

# Asleep, falling sick calls no one, and nothing calls later when it wakes: the moon's strip
# puts it to bed (lights out, the strip's first item), SICK ON while it sleeps, then the moon
# wakes it.
serial SICK OFF
tap 264 404
wait 300ms
tap 231 320
wait 1s
serial SICK ON
wait 2s
expect-not call sick
tap 264 404
wait 2s
expect-not call sick

# E. due to run away (SICK ON 1440) with the screen turned off with PWR, as the player leaves
# it: nobody is looking, so it waits. PWR again (someone looks) and it goes.
press pwr
serial SICK ON 1440
wait 2s
expect-not sick: ran away
serial CARE?
expect * sick=1 * sickmin=1440
press pwr
wait 1s
expect-once sick: ran away
shot 8-runs-away

# F. a call's light alone does not send it away: the pet is left alone on the charger, so the
# standby clock is up (a lit screen nobody has touched, on both boards, by 100 s); SICK OFF
# then SICK ON 1440 is due to run away, and the "sick" and "sick-warning" calls light the
# screen for a moment, which is no one looking: the pet stays. A touch off the pet and the
# buttons (x 400, y 140) is someone looking: it goes. A new pet first (E ended with the
# run-away; the setting is still on): the next egg shows after 20 s, three taps hatch it.
battery 80
wait 20s
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
tap 233 200
wait 6s
usb on
charging on
wait 100s
serial SICK OFF
serial SICK ON 1440
wait 2s
expect-once call sick+sick-warning: *
expect-not sick: ran away
wait 15s
expect-not sick: ran away
serial CARE?
expect * sick=1 * sickmin=14*
tap 400 140
wait 1s
tap 400 140
wait 1s
expect-once sick: ran away
